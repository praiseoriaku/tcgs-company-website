About TCGS

Structural work, built by people who supervise it end to end.

Tropical Concept Global Services has been in existence since 2000 and was formally incorporated in Nigeria on 15th May 2015. We manufacture, fabricate and install aluminium, glass and metal systems for residential, commercial and industrial projects.

With engineers, technicians and computer analysts on staff, we combine practical site experience with quality materials and careful project supervision.

  • Provide adequate and excellent service to every customer
  • Meet client needs at affordable prices
  • Meet the needs of architects and developers directly

How a project moves through TCGS

01

Evaluate & quote

Tender documents, site visits and cost evaluation before production begins.

02

Fabricate in-house

Components are cut, grooved and finished in our workshop, with quality checked against specification.

03

Install & supervise

Site supervisors run installation and safety on-site through to sign-off.

What we build

Every system, fabricated and installed in-house.

A full specification range — from a single casement window to structural glazed curtain walling.

Aluminium doors & windows

Casement, sliding, fixed light and combination units in a range of colours and profiles.

Frameless glass

Shower enclosures, partitions and doors with a clean, hardware-minimal finish.

Dry wall & partitioning

Aluminium partitioning systems for offices and commercial interiors.

Curtain walling

Conventional and structural glazed curtain wall systems for building envelopes.

Spider glass walls

Point-fixed structural glazing for façades and feature walls.

Suspended ceilings

Ceiling systems fabricated and fitted to match interior specification.

Shopfronts & rooflights

Retail-facing glazing and overhead light systems, built for durability.

Aluminium composite panel cladding

ACP cladding for façades, column covers, fascia and canopies.

Balustrades & handrailing

Glass balustrades and stainless steel handrailing for stairs and balconies.
