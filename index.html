About TCGS

Structural work, built by people who supervise it end to end.

Tropical Concept Global Services has been in existence since 2000 and was formally incorporated in Nigeria on 15th May 2015. We manufacture, fabricate and install aluminium, glass and metal systems for residential, commercial and industrial projects.

With engineers, technicians and computer analysts on staff, we combine practical site experience with quality materials and careful project supervision.

  • Provide adequate and excellent service to every customer
  • Meet client needs at affordable prices
  • Meet the needs of architects and developers directly

How a project moves through TCGS

01

Evaluate & quote

Tender documents, site visits and cost evaluation before production begins.

Components are cut, grooved and finished in our workshop.

03

Install & supervise

Site supervisors run installation and safety on-site through to sign-off.
