About TCGS

Structural work, built by people who supervise it end to end.

Tropical Concept Global Services has been in existence since 2000, and was formally incorporated in Nigeria on 15th May 2015 under the Companies and Allied Matters Decree of 1990. We manufacture, fabricate and install aluminium, glass and metal systems for buildings of every scale.

With a staff strength of about fifteen — engineers, technicians and computer analysts — we work with EBM products and techniques, alongside foreign aluminium materials including NIGALEX and TOWEREX, giving clients a choice of finish and performance for every project.

  • Provide adequate and excellent service to every customer
  • Meet client needs at affordable prices
  • Meet the needs of architects and developers directly

How a project moves through TCGS

01
